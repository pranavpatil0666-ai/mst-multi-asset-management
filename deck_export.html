THE REAL WORLD PROBLEM

The 3 Big Problems We Solve

Important real-world documents are easy to fake, double-use, or sell illegally today.

PROBLEM 1
Fake College Degrees

People create fake paper or PDF degrees to get jobs. Normal crypto tokens can be sold to anyone—which is bad because a student should never be able to sell their degree to someone else!

Degrees Cannot Be Resold
PROBLEM 2
Fake Carbon Offsets

Companies claim they helped the environment by buying green credits. But the same credit is often sold or used multiple times because there is no permanent proof of retirement.

Burn Credit = Real Proof
PROBLEM 3
Selling Land Under Bank Loans

Fraudsters sometimes sell land to innocent buyers while the land still has an unpaid bank loan or court dispute. Buyers lose money and get trapped in legal battles.

Lock Land During Loan
EVERYDAY PROBLEMS IN EDUCATION, ENVIRONMENT & REAL ESTATE SLIDE 02 / 11
OUR SOLUTION

Three Smart Rules for Three Types of Assets

Instead of treating everything like a simple crypto coin, we give each asset the exact real-world legal rules it needs.

LOCKED TO STUDENT
College Degrees

When a university issues a degree, it is locked forever to the student's wallet. It can never be transferred or sold, but anyone can verify it instantly online.

BURN TO RETIRE
Carbon Credits

Companies can trade green credits. When ready to claim an offset, the credit is permanently burned on-chain, creating a digital proof receipt that cannot be reused.

BANK LOCK SUPPORT
Land Ownership Titles

Digital property deeds. When an owner takes a bank loan, the land registrar locks the deed. While locked, it cannot be transferred or sold until the loan is paid off!

Only Verified Organizations Can Create Assets

Only government-recognized universities, certified carbon auditors, and official land offices are given permission to issue these documents.

100% Verified Issuance
