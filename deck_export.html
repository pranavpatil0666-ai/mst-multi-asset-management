THE CHALLENGE

The Three Critical Vulnerabilities of Legacy RWAs

Traditional Web3 platforms treat all NFTs identically, failing the strict legal and compliance needs of institutions.

CRISIS 1: FRAUD & FORGERY
Academic Credential Fraud

Fake paper and PDF degrees compromise hiring worldwide. Standard transferable NFTs are catastrophic for diplomas because credentials must be strictly non-transferable (Soulbound) while protecting student privacy under GDPR.

Zero Secondary Sale Allowed
CRISIS 2: DOUBLE-SPENDING
Carbon Credit Greenwashing

Voluntary carbon credit markets are plagued by double-counting. Claiming an offset requires irreversible on-chain burning that produces a non-transferable Proof-of-Offset certificate that cannot be re-traded.

Burn-to-Retire Guarantee
CRISIS 3: LIEN CONFLICTS
Unenforced Land Liens

Real estate title tokens must respect legal encumbrances. When a land parcel is under bank mortgage or judicial dispute, transferability must be cryptographically frozen on-chain to prevent unauthorized sale.

Legal Mortgage Freezes

PROPOSED SOLUTION

Heterogeneous Multi-Standard Asset Engine

Purpose-built smart contracts mapping the precise legal requirements of each asset class into immutable code.

ERC-5192 SOULBOUND
Academic Degree Diplomas

Locked to student wallets upon issuance. Uses Keccak-256 hashed student IDs for privacy and supports cryptographically verified university revocations.

ERC-1155 + SOULBOUND RECEIPT
Carbon Credit Batches

Multi-vintage project batches (1 token = 1 tonne CO2e). Retiring credits permanently burns ERC-1155 units and triggers automatic minting of a Soulbound Proof-of-Offset.

ERC-721 + ENCUMBRANCE
Cadastral Land Titles

Unique GIS parcels with area in sq meters. Municipal registrars can place/lift legal liens (setEncumbrance) which lock transfers during bank mortgages.

Institutional Authority Registry (RBAC)

Only accredited bodies holding UNIVERSITY_ROLE, CARBON_AUDITOR_ROLE, or LAND_REGISTRAR_ROLE can mint or encumber assets.

Zero Unauthorized Minting
